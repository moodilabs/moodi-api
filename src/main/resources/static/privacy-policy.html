Moodi Privacy Policy

Moodi — published by moodilabs · Effective 12 September 2026

moodilabs ("we") operates the Moodi app, which recommends travel spots in Korea by mood. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it.

1. What we collect

CategoryItemsWhen
AccountEmail address and the account identifier supplied by Google or AppleOn sign-in
ProfileNickname, country, birth year, genderDuring onboarding
PreferencesMoods you select in the mood surveyDuring onboarding
Consent recordsTerms of service, privacy policy, age-14-or-over confirmation, marketing opt-in, with timestampsOn consent
PhotosA photo you choose to upload for a photo-based recommendation, and the regions you select with itOnly when you use the photo recommendation feature
Service activityBookmarked spots, routes you create or share, feed view history, recommendation requests and their resultsWhile using the app
TechnicalServer access logs (request time, endpoint, request identifier, error details)Automatically

We do not collect your device location, contacts, payment details, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use your data for advertising profiling.

2. Why we use it

  • Sign-in and account management — account, consent records.
  • Personalised recommendations — preferences, service activity, and the photo you upload.
  • Providing app features — bookmarks, route creation and sharing.
  • Operating and fixing the service — technical logs, abuse prevention.
  • Marketing messages — only if you opted in; you can withdraw at any time.

We process this data to perform the service you asked for and on the consent you give during onboarding. Withdrawing consent is always possible and does not affect processing already carried out.

3. How photos are handled

  • Your photo is uploaded directly to a private cloud storage bucket. It is never made public and has no public URL.
  • To generate a recommendation, the photo is sent to OpenAI through a short-lived signed link and analysed for mood. It is not used to train any model.
  • We keep the photo and the resulting recommendation so you can re-open past results in the app. You can ask us to delete them at any time (section 7).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We use the following processors:

ProviderPurposeData
Google Cloud PlatformApplication hosting, database, photo storage (Seoul region, asia-northeast3)All service data
Google / AppleSocial sign-inEmail address, account identifier
OpenAIAnalysing an uploaded photo for moodThe photo you upload
KakaoTravel time and distance between spots on a routeSpot coordinates only — no personal data

Service data is stored in the Republic of Korea. Photo analysis by OpenAI is processed outside Korea, in the United States, for the duration of the analysis. We also disclose data where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

DataRetention
Account, profile, preferences, consent recordsUntil you withdraw from the service
Photos and recommendation resultsUntil you ask us to delete them, or you withdraw
Social account identifierKept after withdrawal so signing in again restores your bookmarks and routes
Bookmarks, routes, feed view historyKept after withdrawal, no longer linked to your name, nickname, or email
Authentication tokensDeleted on sign-out or withdrawal
Encrypted backupsUp to 30 days, then overwritten by backup rotation

If you would rather leave nothing behind for restoration, tell us and we will erase the remaining records too. See Account Deletion for the full detail.

6. Security

  • All traffic runs over HTTPS.
  • Uploaded photos sit in a private bucket reachable only through short-lived signed links.
  • Access to production data is limited to the people who operate the service.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to show you the data we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop processing it, and you may withdraw any consent you gave. Email moodikr.2026@gmail.com and we will respond within 7 business days. You can also delete your account in the app, or follow Account Deletion.

8. Children

Moodi is not for children under 14. We ask you to confirm you are 14 or over at sign-up and do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date, and notify you in the app before a material change takes effect.

10. Contact

moodilabs — data protection contact: moodikr.2026@gmail.com
